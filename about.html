ABOUT AHMAD

A career built at the intersection of technology, leadership, and continuous evolution.

With over a decade of professional experience spanning graphic design, multimedia production, mobile development, software engineering, and enterprise database administration, Ahmad Fiqi Efendi represents a rare breed of IT professional — one who has evolved with the industry while staying grounded in fundamentals.

His career began in visual communication, where he developed a sharp eye for design systems and user experience. That foundation naturally led into video production and multimedia, deepening his understanding of digital storytelling and interactive content. A pivotal transition into Android development marked his entry into software engineering, where he discovered a passion for building systems at scale.

Today, Ahmad specializes in enterprise database administration for Oracle and MySQL environments, leading IT projects that demand both technical depth and strategic coordination. His work at PT. Infomedia Nusantara serving Maybank's database infrastructure reflects his commitment to reliability, performance, and operational excellence. Beyond his core discipline, he actively explores AI, large language models, and automation — integrating these capabilities into real-world workflows and side projects.
