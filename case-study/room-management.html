Room Management Application

CategoryManagement / Extracurricular
TypeAndroid Application
StatusCOMPLETED
01Overview

A room management application built for organizing extracurricular (ekskul) activities. It helps coordinators allocate and track rooms used by student clubs and activities, keeping schedules and usage clear in one place.

02Problem

Extracurricular activities often compete for limited rooms, leading to overlaps and confusion about who uses what space and when. Coordinators needed a simple way to manage room allocation for ekskul sessions without manual booking sheets.

03Solution

We built an Android application in Java focused on managing rooms for extracurricular activities — tracking assignments, availability, and usage so clubs and organizers can coordinate spaces efficiently. The scope is activity-room management, not hospitality or hotel booking.

04Technology
AndroidJava
05Result

The application was completed and delivered, providing a practical tool for managing extracurricular room usage.
